Prewedding concept brief

Classic at
the Courtyard

A bright neoclassical manor day: white arches, fountain lawn, dark wood doors, and a white kitchen. Three wardrobe changes. Same cream suit. Intimate, editorial, and a little playful.

01 — Space

Studio layout

Use the floor plan as a shot map. Formal looks live in the courtyard, hall, lounge, and bedroom. Aprons stay in the kitchen.

Outdoor · 4.7m garden Courtyard fountain Twirl, lift, framed doorway portraits against white arches and evergreens.
Main hall · 3.7 × 5.9m Columns & lounge Scalloped arches, striped sofa, running through the white archway.
Kitchen · 3.7 × 3.4m White manor kitchen Look 03 only: island, farmhouse sink, gold hardware, window light.
Bedroom · 3.7 × 3m Canopy suite Dark-wood double doors, white linens, quiet close portraits.

03 — Wardrobe

Three looks

Change order: ivory formal → sage formal → kitchen. Keep hair and makeup consistent so the day reads as one story.

Look 01

Ivory formal

  • Women: white silk / satin gown, low bun
  • Men: tailored cream suit, open or simple tie
  • Bouquet: white lily or blush tulip
  • Zones: courtyard, hall, lounge
Look 02

Sage formal

  • Women: sage-green satin gown
  • Men: same cream suit, fresh shirt
  • Bouquet: white roses, sage foliage
  • Zones: fountain, lounge dip, bedroom doors
Look 03

Kitchen story

  • Women: white tank top + linen apron
  • Men: white shirt + matching apron
  • Props: cookbook, citrus, wooden spoon
  • Zone: kitchen island and window only

Look 01

White gown · cream suit

Classic and ceremonial. Prioritize gown movement on the lawn, then quieter frames through the dark-wood doors.

Look 02

Sage gown · cream suit

Same architecture, cooler cloth. Let the green read against white plaster, fountain stone, and cream wool.

Look 03

White tank, shirt & aprons

Home chapter. Keep it bright and tactile: marble, gold hardware, flour-dust hands, the farmhouse sink behind them.

06 — Alternatives

More poses, same rooms

Only the six Manor C Classic rooms from the layout sheet. No new set, no redesigned architecture — just other body language.

Courtyard fountain

Ivory or sage. Use the grass, fountain rim, recessed wall arches, and potted evergreens as they are.

  • Stand side by side, both look at the fountain
  • One looks at the fountain, one looks at partner
  • Slow walk around the fountain, hands behind them
  • She sits on the rim, he stands and cups her face
  • He sits, she stands between his knees
  • Foreheads together, eyes closed, still
  • He kisses her temple while she holds the bouquet
  • Lean on opposite sides of the fountain, reach across
  • Walk toward the camera down the grass path
  • Spin once, then settle into a back hug
  • She hides behind an evergreen urn, peeks out
  • Ring hands in front of the fountain, faces out of frame

Lounge · columns

Striped sofa, two round ottomans, three white columns. Ivory or sage.

  • She sits on the sofa, he sits on the floor at her knees
  • Both on the sofa, she tucked under his arm
  • He lies back, she leans across his chest (tasteful)
  • Sit-and-sway: slow shoulder rock for motion blur
  • She sits on an ottoman, he stands behind fixing her hair
  • Both perched on ottomans, knees touching
  • Lean opposite columns, walk in and meet in the middle
  • She against a column, he adjusts her earring
  • Whisper a secret on the sofa, catch the laugh
  • Standing forehead touch between two columns
  • He kneels to straighten her hem, she looks down
  • Detail: hands and bouquet on the striped sofa

Dining · dark-wood doors

Round table, two chairs, chandelier, framed paintings, double doors. Ivory or sage.

  • Both seated, foreheads almost touching over the table
  • Hands meet in the center, faces looking down
  • She sits, he pours water or sets a flower on the plate
  • Stand behind her chair, kiss the top of her head
  • Walk in through the double doors, pause in the threshold
  • One inside, one outside the doors, holding hands through
  • Close the doors halfway, peek through the gap
  • Slow dance in the small space beside the table
  • She sits on the table edge (careful), he stands close
  • Look at the framed paintings, then at each other

Bedroom

Shoot through the open dark-wood doors. White canopy bed as-is. Ivory or sage.

  • Sit on the bed edge, shoulders touching, look to the doors
  • She sits, he stands in the doorway watching her
  • Both standing in the doorway, forehead touch
  • She on the bed, he sits on the floor holding her hand
  • Walk in, she looks back over her shoulder
  • Lie on the bed from the waist up only, facing each other
  • He sits against the headboard, she leans on his chest
  • Canopy as a frame: shoot from the foot of the bed
  • Detail: rings on white linen
  • Stand at the pedestal tables, adjust each other’s clothes

Bathroom

Vanity, marble, sconces, clawfoot tub. Formal looks only. Stay fully clothed.

  • Both at the mirror, he fastens her necklace
  • She applies lipstick, he watches from the door
  • Back hug, both looking into the mirror, then at each other
  • She sits on the closed tub edge, he leans on the vanity
  • Forehead kiss reflected in the mirror
  • Hands and rings on the marble, mirror soft in the back
  • She sits, gown pooling on the mosaic floor
  • Tight frame of faces in the mirror only

Kitchen · Look 03 only

White shaker, gold hardware, farmhouse sink, range hood. Tank, shirt, and aprons. Do not shoot formal gowns here.

  • Make tea or coffee, pass the cup
  • Flour-dust a heart on the marble, then laugh it off
  • She sits on the island, he stands between her knees
  • Chop herbs side by side, bump hips
  • He ties her apron, she ties his
  • Lean on opposite sides of the island, talk
  • Wash dishes, splash, wipe her cheek
  • Taste from the spoon, then a forehead kiss
  • She reads a cookbook, he cooks behind her
  • Window light: both lean on the sink, look out, then in
  • Detail: gold knobs, citrus, two pairs of hands
  • Slow dance in the galley, wooden spoon still in hand
